## What you'll be able to do

- Take a commit through CI, the registry and OpenTofu to production without a
  manual step
- Find a fault from its symptoms when nobody says which layer it is in
- Hold a latency and SLO target while the system is under a chaos experiment
- Write the postmortem and runbook for what happened

## The sandbox

Several stacks at once: `ci-stack` for the forge, runner and registry, and
`ha-stack` for the load balancer pair the service runs behind. Later stages
add `chaos-stack` and `obs-stack`. Each stage starts every stack it needs.

## Before you start

Modules 11, 12, 13, 18, 21, 22 and 24. The capstone stages are not counted in
the exercise totals.
