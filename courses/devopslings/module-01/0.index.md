## What you'll be able to do

- Read a full-disk situation correctly when `df` and `du` disagree
- Work Brendan Gregg's 60-second checklist instead of guessing at `top`
- Diagnose a failed systemd unit from its journal rather than its exit code

## Why these three

Every one of them is a scenario where the intuitive fix makes the symptom go
away for a few seconds and then it comes back. That gap — between the thing
you noticed and the thing that caused it — is the whole subject of this repo,
and it is cheapest to learn on a single box before there are twelve of them.

## Before you start

Nothing. `linux-box` is one container and boots in about a second.
