## What you'll be able to do

- Read Prometheus's target and service-discovery pages and say why a target
  is not scraped, down, or silently dropped
- Instrument a service for rate, errors and duration, in the units and
  buckets that make the numbers mean something
- Route, group and inhibit alerts so one outage sends one page
- Pick `rate` and `increase` over arithmetic on raw counters, and know why a
  restart breaks the arithmetic
- Write an alert that fires when the data it watches disappears, without
  paging on every deploy
- Find the label that multiplied a metric's series and remove it without
  losing the question it was added to answer

## The sandbox

`obs-stack` runs Prometheus, Alertmanager and Grafana next to a handful of
small Python services: a shop and its database, an order API, two queue
workers, a backup job and a load generator. A webhook sink stands in for the
pager and records every notification it gets.

You work in the `box` container. Everything you edit is under `/obs`:

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `/obs/prometheus/prometheus.yml` | scrape config |
| `/obs/prometheus/rules/*.yml` | alerting and recording rules |
| `/obs/alertmanager/alertmanager.yml` | routing, grouping, inhibition |
| `/obs/apps/*.py` | the services' source; saving a file restarts that service |
| `/obs/logs/*.log` | each service's output |

Tools on the PATH: `promq 'EXPR'` runs a query, `obs-reload` makes Prometheus
and Alertmanager re-read their config and tells you if either refuses it,
`promtool` and `amtool` are the real CLIs. Prometheus is at
<http://localhost:19090>, Alertmanager at <http://localhost:19093> and Grafana
at <http://localhost:13000> from your browser.

Everything runs fast here. Prometheus scrapes every 5 seconds and evaluates
rules every 5 seconds; Alertmanager waits 2 seconds before a first
notification. Production is usually 15 to 60 seconds for both, and minutes
for Alertmanager. The ideas are the same at either speed.
